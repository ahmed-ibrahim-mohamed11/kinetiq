# PHASE6_ADDENDA.md — dated addenda to the Phase 6 pre-registration

Rules (docs/PHASE6_PREREGISTRATION.md §0, §3.4, §7):
- Append-only. Entries are never edited or deleted after they are committed.
- Each entry starts with a level-2 heading of the form `## A6-NN — YYYY-MM-DD — title` (NN = 01, 02, …),
  followed by the reason, what changes, and who decided. These headings are the only level-2
  headings in this file; the runner lists them in every report.
- Allowed: clarifying examples for the annotation guide after a failed agreement round; documenting
  an evaluation-code fix (the bug, the diff and the test) before a re-run with
  `--evaluation-fix A6-NN`; recording an environment repair; explaining a change of second annotator.
- Not allowed: changing any rule — a gate, a threshold, a definition (START / PULL / END), a
  confidence tier, a coverage or data-quality rule, an exclusion or abstention rule.
- No names: annotators are `a1`, `a2`, …; athletes are `A01`, `A02`, ….
