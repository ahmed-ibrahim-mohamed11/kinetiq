## P2.7 plan — last pre-freeze improvement (pre-registered 2026-10-10, before any candidate number)

Owner instruction (2026-10-10): ONE bounded, evidence-gated attempt on R-07 (side view) and R-08
(paused tempo), using only existing licensed development data and LOSO; the locked TEST split is
never touched; if no gain passes the gate, the weakness is documented as a known limitation and
nothing more is tuned.

**Weakness.** R-07: LOSO side-view IoU F1 **0.6537** (236/137/113) vs front 0.9671 and diagonal
0.9738 (P2.6); locked TEST side 0.383. As a dev-data analogue, the frozen Domain C per-view gate
(≥ 0.70) would fail on side view. R-08: P2.2a probes (END hold 0.5 s: internal_sr 0.720,
mendeley_val 0.764).

**Label-free evidence for the hypothesis** (no ground truth, no F1; per-video medians over frames;
n = cross(lat, up) as computed in `prepare_video`; a joint "on the −n side" means
(joint − reference)·n < 0):

| set / view | videos | camera rule picks fwd = +n | knees (vs hips) on the −n side | hands (vs shoulders) on the −n side | median abs(n_z) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Mendeley dev, diagonal | 36 | 35 | 36 | 36 | 0.57 |
| Mendeley dev, front | 37 | 36 | 37 | 34 (3 within 0.07 torso lengths of zero) | 0.88 |
| Mendeley dev, side | 36 | **10** | 36 | 36 | **0.21** |
| internal_sr (rear, product) | 6 | **0** | 6 | 6 | 1.00 |
| external units | 5 | 4 | 5 | 5 | 0.63 |

MotionBERT-Lite's skeletons have the same handedness in every view, so n is the athlete's
anatomical BACKWARD direction everywhere (knees 120/120, hands 117/120). The camera rule
(`fwd = n · sign(median(n·Z))`, i.e. "the athlete faces away from the camera") gives front and
diagonal videos one convention, the rear product view the other, and side videos a near coin flip,
because the camera axis is almost perpendicular to the body's forward axis there. The training data
therefore mixes two conventions (81 videos vs 28), and the product's rear view falls in the
minority one, consistent with P2.1's yaw sweep (internal_sr 0.8406 → 0.9667 when its sign flips).
Why this is not P2.1 again: P2.1 chose the sign per video by the frozen model's own Viterbi
likelihood and did not retrain; it was rejected because the likelihood is not a trustworthy
arbiter. P2.7 uses no model output, no labels and no view metadata — a fixed geometric convention —
and refits on consistent features.

**Change (one line in `prepare_video`).** `fwd = np.cross(up, lat)` (anatomical forward of a
non-mirrored skeleton) instead of `fwd = np.cross(lat, up) · sign(median(… @ FACING_IN_CAMERA))`.
Every feature becomes invariant to camera rotation. Everything else stays frozen: the Phase 2.7 cfg
(no re-selection), splits, masked supervision, decoder, guards, form thresholds. Exactness: the
candidate equals the frozen features with the backward columns negated exactly where the camera
rule chose +n, and is byte-identical elsewhere (asserted for every video in the run).
Rear-view (internal_sr) features are byte-identical (6/6 already −n). The form indicators use
ranges and lags of the backward signal, so they are sign-invariant.

**Pre-registered gates — ACCEPT only if ALL hold:**
- **Validity** (not a gate): the BEFORE arm (same script, camera rule) must reproduce the frozen
  fitted-system fingerprint `63d36c31…`, the P2.6 LOSO numbers (pooled 0.8779, side 0.6537) and all
  8 headline metrics. Otherwise the comparison is INVALID (fix the harness, not the hypothesis).
- **G1** (owner's primary gate): LOSO side-view IoU F1 **≥ 0.70**.
- **G2**: no view's LOSO IoU F1 drops by more than 0.03 (front ≥ 0.9371, diagonal ≥ 0.9438).
- **G3**: LOSO pooled IoU F1 and pull F1 each drop by no more than 0.03 (0.8779 / 0.8996).
- **G4** (global rule 1, frozen regression suites): mendeley_val, internal_sr, external (IoU and
  pull F1) and causal V1/V2/V3 (IoU F1). No F1 drops by more than 0.03, unless the change is at
  most one rep (TP down by ≤ 1 and FP up by ≤ 1), which is noise under rule 4. External stays a
  frozen validation check: nothing is chosen to improve it.
- **G5** (R-08 non-regression): the four P2.2a probes (END hold 0.5 s and 1.0 s, PULL hold 1.0 s,
  uniform 0.7× slowdown) on internal_sr and mendeley_val, same rule as G4.
- **G6**: once the accepted rule is implemented in `ai/core`, the product path reproduces the
  candidate arm's numbers and the full test suite is green. Tests that lock the OLD camera-sign

- **R-08 secondary claim** (reported, not a gate): R-08 counts as improved only if both END-hold

- **Informational** (no gate): CMU Panoptic real-3D check, the correlation of the predicted backward
  signal with the ground-truth backward signal per view under both rules. Prediction: all three
  views positive under the anatomical rule (camera rule: front −0.926, side −0.708, back +0.776).

**If ACCEPTED**: the rule moves into `ai/core/features.py`; the fitted system is exported as a NEW

## How to add a row

1. State the weakness with evidence (an artifact, a failing case, a gap in the risk register).
2. Write the hypothesis and the PRE-REGISTERED accept/reject criteria *before* running anything.
3. Make the minimal change; run the relevant `ai/evaluation/harness.py` suite(s) BEFORE vs AFTER
   on identical data/protocol, plus the full test suite.
4. Record the actual before/after numbers (not a summary) and the decision. A result that beats
   baseline by less than noise (the small-dev-set caution, global rule 4) is REJECTED, not
   "marginally accepted."
5. One row = one commit (global rule 8). Cross-link DECISION_LOG.md if the decision also changes
   a standing architectural choice (e.g. which model is product-primary).
