## What this is

GYM-AI is an academic graduation project: software that watches a video of a seated cable row and
marks where each repetition starts, peaks and ends, with simple form notes. Before it can be called
reliable it must be tested on people it has never seen. You are invited to be one of them.

Your recordings are used **only to test** the software, once, under rules fixed in advance. They are
**not** used to train or adjust it.

## What you will do

- About [20–30] minutes at [place], on [date].
- About five sets of seated cable rows at different speeds (normal, slow, with pauses, fast), plus
  one set with a deliberate, safe form fault (for example pulling unevenly). Rest between sets as
  long as you like; stop at any time.
- Up to three phones record you at the same time: from behind, from the side and from about 45°.
  Your whole body is in the picture; **your face may be visible** from the side and 45° views.

## Who sees the recordings

- The project owner, who marks the repetitions (annotator "a1").
- One second annotator ("a2"), who marks about one video in ten to check the markings.
- [The project supervisor, only if needed to verify the process.]

Nobody else. The recordings are not shown in presentations or reports.

## Where they are kept

On the project owner's laptop only, in a local folder that is **not synced to any cloud service**.
They are not uploaded, not emailed and not shared. You are identified by a code (A01, A02, …); the
list linking codes and names, and this form, are kept separately and offline.

Any recording that accidentally shows another person is deleted once its quality check is recorded.

## What is published

The project report and its code repository publish **only numbers**: how well the software did
overall and for each code (for example "A03: 9 of 10 repetitions found"), repetition timings in
frames, and form-indicator values. **No video, image, face, name or body-pose data is published.**

## Withdrawing

Taking part is voluntary. You can withdraw at any time, without giving a reason:
- **before the evaluation is run** ([planned date of the data lock]): all your recordings and
  markings are deleted and nothing about you is used;
- **after the evaluation has run**: your recordings and derived data are deleted on request, but the
  anonymous numbers already computed and published remain, because removing them would invalidate a
  test whose rules were fixed in advance.
